Non-negotiable risk rules
This is an educational curriculum, not a profit guarantee or personalized investment recommendation. Begin with NSE cash-equity charts and paper trading. Do not target a fixed daily percentage. Define risk before entry; include brokerage, taxes and slippage in results. Stop when your predefined loss limit is reached. SMC labels such as Order Block or FVG do not prove that institutions placed orders there. DOM shows displayed liquidity, not hidden intent. Futures and options can magnify losses; study them only after building and testing a process in simulation.
